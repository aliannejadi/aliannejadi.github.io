I am an assistant professor at the [Information Retrieval Lab (IRLab)](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/) of the University of Amsterdam, working on **conversational and agentic information access**: systems that search, reason over retrieved evidence, and hold a dialogue with their users, asking for clarification when a need is unclear. Running through all of this is a question I care deeply about: **how do we know these systems actually work?** Much of my research builds the test collections, benchmarks, and user simulators needed to answer it, and studies when large language models can, and cannot, be trusted as judges.

Current directions in my group include:

- **Conversational search and mixed-initiative interaction**: clarifying questions, query rewriting, and retrieval-augmented generation in multi-turn settings.
- **Evaluation with and of LLMs**: LLM-based relevance judgments, reusable test collections, and evaluating deep research agents.
- **User simulation**: simulating users to train and evaluate interactive systems, and running shared tasks around simulators.
- **Query performance prediction** and **recommender systems**, including cross-market and next-basket recommendation.

I am passionate about building shared resources and bringing the community together around open problems. I lead the [TREC Interactive Knowledge Assistance Track (iKAT)](https://www.trecikat.com/), and before that led the mixed-initiative subtask of [TREC CAsT](https://www.treccast.ai/), chaired the ClariQ challenge (ConvAI3), and co-organized the IGLU competition at NeurIPS. With my students and collaborators I have released datasets and benchmarks such as Total Recall QA, the iKAT collections, ClariQ, Qulac, ANTIQUE, and XMarket (see [data & code]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }})). I enjoy organizing venues where these conversations happen: workshops such as SCAI, LLM4Eval, QPP++, and Eval4RAG, the European Summer School in IR (ESSIR 2024), and roles as program co-chair of ICTIR 2023 and lab co-chair of CLEF 2023.

Before joining IRLab as a faculty member, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam. I obtained my Ph.D. from the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) in Lugano, Switzerland, with a thesis on modeling user information needs on mobile devices, including a research visit to [CIIR](https://ciir.cs.umass.edu/) at UMass Amherst. Before that, I completed my M.Sc. at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic).

**Prospective students:** I am always happy to hear from motivated students interested in conversational search, LLM-based evaluation, or user simulation. Open positions are announced on the [IRLab website](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/category/vacancy/). Current UvA M.Sc. students who want to do a thesis with me can email me with a short description of their interests.

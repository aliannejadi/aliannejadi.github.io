I am an assistant professor at the [IRLab](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/) of the University of Amsterdam, working on **conversational and agentic information access**. I focus on building and evaluating systems that search and reason over retrieved evidence while interacting with a user in multimodal and mixed-initiative environments. One central question I ask is how to benchmark and evaluate such systems, and to what extent we can trust LLMs-as-judges and user simulators.

Current directions in my work include:

- **Conversational and agentic search**: retrieval-augmented generation and reasoning over multi-turn conversations, efficient conversational retrieval, and deep research agents.
- **Mixed-initiative and multimodal interaction**: asking clarifying questions, multimodal clarification, and multimodal RAG systems.
- **Benchmarking and evaluation**: test collections such as TREC iKAT and Total Recall QA, and the reliability of LLMs-as-judges for relevance and response quality.
- **User simulation**: simulating users to train and evaluate interactive systems, and infrastructure for running simulation-based shared tasks.
- **Query performance prediction, recommendation, and fairness**: predicting retrieval quality without judgments, cross-market and next-basket recommendation, and bias in ranking.

I am passionate about building shared resources and bringing the community together around open problems. I lead the [TREC Interactive Knowledge Assistance Track (iKAT)](https://www.trecikat.com/), and before that led the mixed-initiative subtask of [TREC CAsT](https://www.treccast.ai/), chaired the ClariQ challenge (ConvAI3), and co-organized the IGLU competition at NeurIPS. With my students and collaborators I have released datasets and benchmarks such as Total Recall QA, the iKAT collections, ClariQ, Qulac, ANTIQUE, and XMarket (see [data & code]({{ '/resources/' | relative_url }})). I enjoy organizing venues where these conversations happen: workshops such as SCAI, LLM4Eval, QPP++, and Eval4RAG, the European Summer School in IR (ESSIR 2024), and roles as program co-chair of ICTIR 2023 and lab co-chair of CLEF 2023.

Before joining IRLab as a faculty member, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Amsterdam. I obtained my Ph.D. from the Università della Svizzera italiana (USI) in Lugano, Switzerland, with a thesis on modeling user information needs on mobile devices, including a research visit to [CIIR](https://ciir.cs.umass.edu/) at UMass Amherst. Before that, I completed my M.Sc. at Amirkabir University of Technology (Tehran Polytechnic).

**Prospective students:** I am always happy to hear from motivated students interested in conversational and agentic search, multimodal RAG, evaluation with LLMs, or user simulation. Open positions are announced on the [IRLab website](https://irlab.science.uva.nl/category/vacancy/). Current UvA M.Sc. students who want to do a thesis with me can email me with a short description of their interests.
